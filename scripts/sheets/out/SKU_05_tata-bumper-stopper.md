## 03 Rubber compound options [🛑 The load compound is missing]
> live: LIVE → Neoprene, SBR — two of the eight · natural rubber, the compound a suspension stop is normally made from, is not offered
- **All eight, always — and the omission here is the important one**:
  > Margo produces every part in every compound it mixes (client-instructed 2026-09-02). The live selector offers two, understating the offer by six.
  > 🛑 On a load-bearing suspension stop, natural rubber is not a preference — it is the normal answer. Highest fatigue life and lowest creep of the eight, which is exactly what a part that carries a laden chassis and then absorbs the strike needs. It is not on this page.
  > And SBR, which is offered, is wrong twice over here: lower fatigue life than natural rubber, and poor ozone resistance on a part bolted to a chassis that lives outdoors in monsoon and dust. Ozone cracking is the documented failure mode of a solid rubber stop.
- **Selector** (Rank | Compound | Temperature | Hardness | For this part):
