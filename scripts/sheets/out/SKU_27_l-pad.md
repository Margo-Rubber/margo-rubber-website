## 03 Rubber compound options [🛑 The load-pad compound is missing]
> live: LIVE → EPDM (SELECTED), NBR, Neoprene, SBR — natural rubber absent on a load-bearing pad
- **Wrong default, a missing option, and one point specific to the shape**:
  > 🛑 Natural rubber is not offered, and it has the best fatigue resistance, highest resilience and least creep of the eight — which is what a pad holding a gap for years is judged on.
  > 🛑 EPDM is pre-selected. It weathers well and creeps more under sustained load, and on a pad whose job is to hold a gap, creep is the failure.
  > 🛑 And the angle changes one ranking. An internal corner concentrates stress, so tear strength matters more on this shape than on a flat pad — which moves silicone down and natural rubber, the best of the eight for tear, further up.
  > Margo produces every part in every compound it mixes (client-instructed 2026-09-02). Nothing is removed — all eight are ranked.
- **Selector** (Rank | Compound | Temperature | Why, for an angled packing pad):
  | Recommended | Natural rubber | −20 to +90 °C | Best fatigue resistance, least creep and the best tear strength of the eight — and tear strength matters here, because an internal corner is where an angled section splits. Not for oil, and it perishes in prolonged sunlight |
  | Recommended | Neoprene | −40 to +120 °C | Where the pad is exposed to weather or sees oil. Good fatigue life and reasonable tear strength, with better ozone resistance than natural rubber |
  | Suitable | SBR | −30 to +100 °C | Low-cost enclosed packing. Fatigue and tear below natural rubber, and no oil resistance |
  | Suitable | EPDM | −40 to +140 °C | Weather, ozone and steam. Creeps more than natural rubber, so allow for it in the deflection |
  | Suitable | NBR | −30 to +120 °C | Where the pad sits in oil. Poor ozone resistance, so not for an exposed corner |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | Silicone | −60 to +220 °C | Lowest tear strength of the eight, and an internal corner is a tear origin. Specify only where temperature leaves no choice |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | FKM · AEM | See the compound table | Expensive sealing compounds with no fatigue, creep or tear advantage in a packing pad |
  _Live set: EPDM (SELECTED), NBR, Neoprene, SBR — four of eight, unranked, and the standard answer is not among them.
The ranking here is driven by tear strength as well as creep, which is what makes it different from the ranking on a flat pad — and it is why silicone drops to not recommended on this shape while it stays merely available on a closed one_

  - Feed material as natural rubber, neoprene and EPDM — the three genuinely recommended.
