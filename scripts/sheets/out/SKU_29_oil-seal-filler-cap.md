## 03 Rubber compound options [🛑 Two of the four fail in oil]
> live: LIVE → EPDM (SELECTED), NBR, Neoprene, SBR — identical to the twin URL, including the selection
- **The same four compounds, the same wrong default**:
  > 🛑 EPDM is pre-selected on a cap for an oil filler. It swells badly in mineral oil, and the cap lives in hot oil vapour. A buyer who does not touch the control is quoted a part that softens, swells and stops sealing.
  > 🛑 SBR has no oil resistance either. Half the menu fails in the medium the product is named after.
  > This block is also what makes the two URLs indistinguishable. Both show the same four compounds with the same one selected, so there is no state in which the two pages present a buyer with anything different.
  > Margo produces every part in every compound it mixes (client-instructed 2026-09-02). Nothing is removed — all eight are ranked, with reasons on the two that fail.
- **Selector** (Rank | Compound | Temperature | Why, for an oil filler cap):
  | Recommended | NBR | −30 to +120 °C | The standard oil-service compound and the cheapest sensible choice for engine oil, gear oil and hydraulic fluid. This is what should be pre-selected |
  | Recommended | AEM | −40 to +175 °C
continuous to +160 °C | Where the oil runs hotter than NBR can take. Costs far less than FKM and is the step most suppliers never offer |
  | Suitable | FKM | −20 to +250 °C
continuous to +200 °C | Hot oil and aggressive fluids, at 2–3× the cost of NBR. Specify it against a measured temperature |
  | Suitable | Neoprene | −40 to +120 °C | Moderate oil resistance with better weathering, for a filler that is exposed as well as oily |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | EPDM | −40 to +140 °C | Swells badly in mineral oil. Pre-selected on the live page, and the one compound this part must not be made from |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | SBR · natural rubber | See the compound table | No oil resistance at all |
