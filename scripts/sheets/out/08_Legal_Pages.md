  ### Technical Information & Specifications
    Technical information on this site is published to assist engineers in selecting a compound and specifying a component. **It is indicative, based on typical values for the compound families we mix, and is not a specification for any particular part.**
    The governing specification for any order is the approved drawing and the compound designation recorded on the quotation and order acknowledgement. Where anything published here conflicts with those documents, **the order documents govern**.

  ### Material Data & Test Values
    Published temperature ranges, hardness bands, tensile values, compression set figures and physical properties are **typical values for the compound family**, determined in our laboratory under the standards named on the relevant page. Those standards include ASTM D2240 and ISO 48-4 for hardness, ASTM D412 and ISO 37 for tensile and elongation, ASTM D395 and ISO 815-1 for compression set, ASTM D573 and ISO 188 for heat ageing, ASTM D297 and ISO 2781 for specific gravity, and ISO 6502-2 for cure characteristics.
    Ozone resistance, fluid immersion ageing, low-temperature brittleness, abrasion, tear, rebound and flammability testing are carried out through a NABL-accredited partner laboratory rather than in-house, and are available on request.
    **Test values are not performance guarantees.** Actual performance in service depends on the specific formulation, part geometry, cure state, installed compression, service medium, temperature, pressure and duty cycle. A compound that performs to a published range in a laboratory test may behave differently in your assembly.

Specifically, we do not hold ISO 13485 or FDA registration for medical devices, and we do not hold NORSOK M-710 or API elastomer qualification for oil and gas service.
