## 03 Rubber compound options [🛑 SBR crazes in sun · EPDM missing]
> live: LIVE → Neoprene, SBR — two of the eight, presented as equals · EPDM, the automotive glazing standard, is not offered
- **All eight, always — and this page has a live selection error**:
  > Margo produces every part in every compound it mixes (client-instructed 2026-09-02). The live selector offers two, which understates the offer by six.
  > 🛑 Worse, one of the two is wrong for the duty. SBR has poor ozone resistance. This part sits on the outside of the vehicle in direct sunlight for the life of the car, and ozone attack on a stressed rubber surface produces exactly the fine cracking that makes an old windscreen rubber perish. A buyer choosing SBR from an unranked list of two gets a part that crazes.
  > And the right answer is absent. EPDM is the universal automotive glazing compound — weatherstrips, glass runs, door seals — precisely because it resists ozone and UV and stays flexible cold. It is not on this page.
