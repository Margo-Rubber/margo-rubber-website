## 03 Rubber compound options [🛑 The pre-selected compound swells in the medium]
> live: LIVE → EPDM (SELECTED), NBR, Neoprene, SBR — AEM absent, EPDM wrong on temperature and oil
- **Wrong default, and the right compound missing**:
  > 🛑 EPDM is pre-selected on a part surrounded by hot oil mist. It reaches +140 °C, which a hot cam cover approaches, and it swells badly in mineral oil. It is the one compound of the eight that should not be anywhere near an engine cover.
  > 🛑 And AEM is not on the menu, though it is exactly the compound for this duty — −40 to +160 °C continuous, formulated for hot oil and oil mist. This is the fifth page in the set where AEM is the right answer and is not offered anywhere on the site.
  > Margo produces every part in every compound it mixes (client-instructed 2026-09-02). Nothing is removed — all eight are ranked, and EPDM stays listed with the reason it is not recommended here.
- **Selector** (Rank | Compound | Temperature | Why, on an engine cover):
  | Recommended | AEM | −40 to +175 °C
continuous to +160 °C | Formulated for hot oil and oil mist, with the temperature headroom a cam cover needs. The compound this part should be quoted in, and it is on no selector on the site |
  | Recommended | Silicone | −60 to +220 °C | Where temperature alone governs and the cover is not oil-wetted. Excellent heat stability, and it damps well soft. Tears more easily and swells in hot mineral oil |
  | Suitable | NBR | −30 to +120 °C | Good oil resistance at lower cost, but the temperature ceiling is the limit on a hot engine |
  | Suitable | Neoprene | −40 to +120 °C | Moderate oil resistance and good general durability, with the same temperature ceiling as NBR |
  | Available | FKM | −20 to +250 °C
continuous to +200 °C | Handles anything an engine bay produces, at 2–3× the cost of NBR and with no damping advantage |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | EPDM | −40 to +140 °C | Swells in mineral oil, and a cam cover lives in oil mist. Offered on the live page and pre-selected there |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | SBR · natural rubber | See the compound table | No oil resistance and no temperature headroom |
  _Live set: EPDM (SELECTED), NBR, Neoprene, SBR — four of eight, unranked, and three of the four are limited by temperature or oil.
AEM leads for the first time anywhere in this catalogue. It has been flagged as the missing middle option on four earlier sheets; here it is not a cheaper alternative to FKM but the correct first choice, and Margo mixes it in-house_
