## 03 Rubber compound options [🛑 A compound menu under a compound heading]
> live: LIVE → EPDM (SELECTED), NBR, Neoprene, SBR — four choices on a page named after one of them
- **Two problems, one of them structural**:
  > 🛑 The heading and the control contradict each other. A buyer who selects NBR on a page called “EPDM Pad” is holding something the page cannot name. Renaming the page to what the part does fixes this in one move — the selector becomes a real choice and EPDM becomes the recommendation.
  > 🛑 Natural rubber is not offered, and on a load-bearing pad it has the best fatigue resistance and the least creep of the eight. EPDM is the right default for weather; it is not the right default for sustained load, and the page cannot say so while it is named after EPDM.
  > Margo produces every part in every compound it mixes (client-instructed 2026-09-02). Nothing is removed — all eight are ranked, and the four missing from the live selector are added.
- **Selector** (Rank | Compound | Temperature | Why, for a flat packing pad):
  | Recommended | EPDM | −40 to +140 °C | The general-purpose answer and correctly pre-selected. Weather, ozone, UV, water and steam, and it stays flexible cold. Creeps more than natural rubber under sustained load, so allow for it in the deflection |
  | Recommended | Natural rubber | −20 to +90 °C | Where the pad carries sustained load. Best fatigue resistance, highest resilience and least creep of the eight, so the gap stays held. Not for oil, and it perishes in prolonged sunlight |
  | Suitable | Neoprene | −40 to +120 °C | The compromise where the pad is both loaded and exposed — good fatigue life with reasonable weathering |
  | Suitable | NBR | −30 to +120 °C | Where the pad sits in oil. Poor ozone resistance, so not for an exposed position |
  | Suitable | SBR | −30 to +100 °C | Low-cost enclosed packing. Fatigue life below natural rubber and no oil resistance |
  | Available | Silicone | −60 to +220 °C | High temperature. Low tear strength and poor fatigue resistance make it a weak choice under load |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | FKM · AEM | See the compound table | Expensive sealing compounds with no fatigue or creep advantage in a packing pad |
  _Live set: EPDM (SELECTED), NBR, Neoprene, SBR — four of eight, unranked.
The closing line does a job specific to this page: it says every compound is available on every pad in the range, which is the misunderstanding the live naming creates.
EPDM and natural rubber are both marked recommended, because the honest answer depends on whether weather or load dominates — and that is a question the page should ask rather than pre-answer_
