## 03 Rubber compound options [🛑 Premium default · ranked, and expanded to eight]
> live: LIVE → FKM (SELECTED), Silicone, EPDM, NBR — the dearest option opens first; NBR is the standard answer
- **It is a pricing problem, not a technical one**:
  > 🛑 FKM is not wrong for an oil filler cap — it is more than most of them need. NBR has been the standard oil-seal compound for decades and handles hot oil and oil vapour at filler-neck temperatures. FKM earns its place where the engine runs hotter than NBR’s +120 °C, and there it is the right call.
  > But FKM costs 2–3× NBR, and on a part ordered in tens of thousands that is the difference between winning and losing the enquiry. Opening the selector on it quotes the premium to everyone.
  > Margo produces every part in every compound it mixes (client-instructed 2026-09-02). Nothing is removed — the eight are ranked, and the default moves to NBR.
- **Selector** (Rank | Compound | Temperature | Why, for a filler cap seal):
  | Recommended | NBR | −30 to +120 °C | The standard oil-seal compound and the right default here. Engine oil, oil vapour and blow-by gases at filler-neck temperature. This is what should be pre-selected |
  | Recommended | FKM | −20 to +250 °C
continuous to +200 °C | Where the measured temperature exceeds NBR’s range — a hot-running or turbocharged engine. 🛑 Costs 2–3× NBR, so specify it on evidence, not by default |
  | Suitable | Silicone | −60 to +220 °C | High temperature without direct oil immersion. 🛑 Silicone swells in hot engine oil — not a first choice on this part |
  | Suitable | AEM | −40 to +175 °C
continuous to +160 °C | The middle answer between NBR and FKM — hot oil-mist service at well under FKM cost |
  | Available | Neoprene | −40 to +120 °C | Moderate oil resistance with better weathering — rarely the best pick here |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | EPDM | −40 to +140 °C | 🛑 EPDM swells badly in mineral oil. It is offered on the live page and it is the wrong compound for an oil seal. Available, but not for this application |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | SBR | −30 to +100 °C | No oil resistance |
  | 🛑 Not recommended | Natural rubber | −20 to +90 °C | No oil resistance |
  _Live set: FKM (SELECTED), Silicone, EPDM, NBR — four of eight, unranked, dearest first, and one of them wrong for oil.
🛑 EPDM is on the live selector of an oil-seal page. Sheet 08 established that EPDM swells in mineral oil; here that is not a caution, it is a misdirection — a buyer who picks it gets a seal that fails. It stays listed, because availability and suitability are different things, and it is marked not recommended with the reason.
The closing line is the one that wins repeat business: a supplier who says “NBR is enough” when it is gets asked back_
