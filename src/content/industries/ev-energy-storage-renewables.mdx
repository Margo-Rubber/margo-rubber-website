  - q: "Do you have UL 94 rated grades?"
    a: "We hold no UL listing. Tell us the rating your drawing names and we confirm whether a compound route exists."
  - q: "Will your gasket make my pack IP67?"
    a: "No gasket can promise that alone. IEC 60529 rates the tested enclosure: the gasket, the lid stiffness, the bolt spacing and the closing force together. Send the lid drawing and we size the section and compression for it."
  - q: "Do you have stock sizes?"
    a: "Made to your drawing. A new part needs a new mould, so send the drawing (or a sample and the mating parts) first."
  - q: "How large a gasket can you mould in one piece?"
    a: "Up to 24 × 24 inches (about 610 × 610 mm). Larger pack and cabinet outlines are made as an extruded profile with joined corners."
  - q: "What is the minimum order for battery-pack and enclosure gaskets?"
    a: "There is no fixed number. The minimum is set by part size and tooling, and we quote it with the part. Send the drawing and your annual volume and you get both figures in one reply. Prototype and first-article lots sit outside the minimum."
