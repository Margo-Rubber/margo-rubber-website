faqs:
  - q: "Do you stock tri-clamp gaskets?"
    a: "No stock range. We mould each size to your ferrule standard and drawing."
  - q: "Which clamp standard should I put on the drawing?"
    a: "The one your ferrules were made to: DIN 32676, ISO 2852 or ASME BPE. The sizes do not all match across the three, and a gasket cut for one can sit proud or short in another."
  - q: "Are your compounds suitable for food contact?"
    a: "We hold no food-contact certificate for any compound, so food-contact compliance stays with your equipment approval. Tell us the product, temperature and cleaning cycle and we choose the compound for that duty."
  - q: "Do you make patient-contact parts?"
    a: "No. Our pharma work is process equipment only."
  - q: "What is the minimum order for food and pharma process seals?"
    a: "There is no fixed number. The minimum is set by part size and tooling, and we quote it with the part. Send the drawing and your annual volume and you get both figures in one reply. Prototype and first-article lots sit outside the minimum."
