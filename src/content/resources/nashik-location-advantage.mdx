## Where Margo is

Our plant is at Vilholi, just off the Mumbai–Agra highway on the Mumbai side of
Nashik. That puts us on the road that already links Nashik to Mumbai, with the
Igatpuri and Sinnar interchanges of the Samruddhi Expressway close by, and the
Nashik–Pune highway starting across the city.

Nashik, Mumbai and Pune have long been called Maharashtra's golden triangle of
industry. For a buyer, the practical meaning is simple: a supplier in Nashik is
within a working day of most of the state's automotive, engineering, pump and
appliance manufacturers, and close enough to Nhava Sheva (JNPT) to ship
export containers without a long inland haul.

## Transit times within Maharashtra

The table below gives typical road transit for a dedicated truck leaving our
gate. Part-load consignments that are consolidated with other cargo can take a
day longer.

| Destination | Industrial areas | Approx. road distance | Typical transit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nashik district | Ambad, Satpur, Sinnar, Gonde, Igatpuri, Dindori | 10 to 45 km | Under 1.5 hours |
| Mumbai Metropolitan Region | Bhiwandi, Thane, Navi Mumbai (TTC), Taloja | 140 to 190 km | 3.5 to 5 hours |
| Nhava Sheva (JNPT) | Export port and container freight stations | about 200 km | 5 to 6 hours |

### Mumbai and the port: same day

Bhiwandi and Thane are the shortest of the long runs. A truck that leaves in
the morning is unloading the same afternoon. Inside Mumbai city, commercial
vehicles are restricted during the day, so deliveries to city addresses are
usually planned for permitted hours or routed through a Bhiwandi warehouse.

The same road continues to Nhava Sheva. Export cargo can be stuffed at our
plant or delivered to a container freight station near the port in a single
day, which is why our export shipments leave through JNPT. See the
[export page](/export) for sea transit times to each market.

### Pune: same day

Pune's automotive and engineering clusters, from Chakan and Bhosari to
Talegaon and Ranjangaon, are reached on the Nashik–Pune highway. A morning
dispatch arrives the same day. For buyers in Pune, that makes a Nashik
supplier a practical second source to one in Pune itself.

### Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and north Maharashtra: half a day

Waluj, Shendra and the newer AURIC estate at Bidkin are about four to five
hours away, either on the state highway through Vaijapur or on the Samruddhi
Expressway. Dhule and Jalgaon are a similar run to the north-east.

### Nagpur: overnight on the Samruddhi Expressway

Nagpur used to be a long two-day haul from western Maharashtra. Since the
Samruddhi Expressway opened end to end, the run from the Nashik district to
Nagpur is a single stretch of expressway. A truck loaded in the evening is
typically at a plant in Butibori, Hingna or MIHAN the next morning.

Maharashtra is our home market, but it is not the edge of it. Nashik sits
roughly in the middle of India's west coast, and the national highways south
and north start a short distance from the plant.

### South India

Bengaluru, Hosur and Chennai make up one of the country's largest automotive
and electronics clusters. From Nashik the route runs south through Pune and
Kolhapur, or east through Hyderabad. A full truck typically takes two to three
days to Bengaluru and Hosur and about three days to Chennai.

### North India

The Delhi NCR belt, from Gurugram and Manesar to Faridabad and Noida, is reached
along the Mumbai–Agra highway or by joining the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway in
Gujarat. Two and a half to three and a half days is typical. Neemrana and
Bhiwadi in Rajasthan are on the way and a little closer.

## What this means when you buy from us

- **Lower safety stock in Maharashtra.** If a part can reach you the same day,
  you do not have to hold weeks of buffer against a missed truck.
- **Faster recovery when something goes wrong.** A short run or a rejected lot
  can be replaced by road within a day across most of the state.
- **Samples and first articles.** Samples to the main metros usually travel by
  courier and arrive in one to two days.
- **Planned dispatch outside Maharashtra.** For buyers in the south and north,
  we agree a dispatch schedule that covers the two to four days on the road, so
  parts arrive when your line needs them.
- **One plant for domestic and export.** The same Nashik plant and the same
  ISO 9001:2015 quality system serve both, with Nhava Sheva a day's drive away.

## A note on these numbers

The times above are typical road transit for a dedicated truck from our gate.
They are not a delivery commitment. Traffic, weather, monsoon conditions on the
ghat sections, city entry rules and the transporter's own schedule all move
them. Part-load consignments can take longer. Your quotation states the
dispatch date and delivery terms for your order, and if you tell us where your
plant or warehouse is, we will give you a realistic figure for that address.
